MedTools Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 28, 2026

1. Overview

MedTools is a medical calculator application designed to provide educational and clinical calculation tools for healthcare professionals and students.

MedTools does not require users to create an account or provide personal information in order to use the basic calculator features.

2. Information You Enter

The calculator values entered by users are used to perform calculations and display results within the application.

MedTools does not intentionally collect names, email addresses, medical records, or other personally identifying information through the basic calculator functions.

3. Medical Information

Users should avoid entering personally identifiable patient information into the application.

MedTools is not designed to store or maintain patient medical records.

4. Advertising and Google AdMob

MedTools may use Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob) to display advertisements.

When advertisements are displayed, Google and its advertising partners may automatically process certain information associated with the device and interaction with advertisements. This may include information such as IP address, advertising or device identifiers, app interactions, and diagnostic or performance information.

This information may be used for purposes including advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, security, and improving advertising services.

For more information about how Google uses data when you use its partners' apps and services, please visit:

How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

5. Data Security

MedTools does not intentionally maintain a database containing users' personal medical information through the basic calculator features.

Information processed by third-party advertising services is handled according to the applicable policies and security practices of those services.

6. Third-Party Services

MedTools may use third-party services such as Google AdMob for advertising. These services may collect and process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Users can review Google's privacy information here:

7. Children's Privacy

MedTools is not specifically directed toward children. We do not intentionally collect personal information from children through the application.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the application, advertising services, or applicable requirements. Updated versions will be published on this page.

قد تُستخدم هذه المعلومات لأغراض تشمل عرض الإعلانات والتحليلات ومنع الاحتيال وتحسين الأمان وتحسين خدمات الإعلانات.
